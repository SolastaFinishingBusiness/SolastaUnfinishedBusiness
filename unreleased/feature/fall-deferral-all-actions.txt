- "Let a creature fall only after all forced movements from a single action has been resolved" (Gameplay > Rules, on by default) now covers every action, not only Eldritch Blast with Repelling Blast or Grasp of Hadar. A creature that the pushes and pulls of one spell, power, attack or shove move over a drop falls only once that action has no more of them to come, from where the last one leaves it. One action is one cast or power use, or one attack with what its hit sets off (for example Gravity Well, Thunderous Strike or Weapon Mastery Push); each attack of an Extra Attack is its own action, and so is a reaction. Teleports, swaps and grapple drags are not covered. With the setting on, the creature also travels through those pushes and pulls without its falling pose and getting up; a push from anything else plays as before. Off: as before.
- Not tested in game: anything on two computers; Gravity Well rays split between creatures; a reaction push in the middle of an action, a push that also knocks prone, a blocked last move or a large creature.
