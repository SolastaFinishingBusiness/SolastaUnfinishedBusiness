- New setting (on by default), Gameplay > Rules: one concentration saving throw per damage instance against its total damage, instead of one per damage type. An attack with all its riders (including rider powers such as Spike Barrage), one hit of a spell or power, entering or starting a turn in an area, or one recurring tick is one instance; each Magic Missile dart, Radiant Motes mote and Eldritch Blast beam counts on its own. The DC counts the damage taken before temporary hit points absorb any of it, and monsters' concentration follows the same rule. Dropping to 0 hit points still ends concentration at once.
- New setting, Gameplay > Rules (on with the 2024 rules): the DC of concentration saving throws is capped at 30.
- Concentration saves no longer get bonuses tied to the damage type: Dwarven Resilience, the Belt of Dwarvenkind and similar poison advantages used to apply to concentration saves for poison damage. War Caster and other concentration bonuses still apply.
- Not tested in game: all of the above; not tested on two computers.
