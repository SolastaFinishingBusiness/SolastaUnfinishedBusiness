- New setting (on by default), Gameplay > Rules: one concentration saving throw per damage instance against its total damage, instead of one per damage type. An attack with all the damage it adds on the hit (smites, Sneak Attack and the like), one hit of a spell or power, entering or starting a turn in an area, or one recurring tick is one instance (a power a hit sets off afterwards, such as Spike Barrage, is its own; each cell moved through Spike Growth is its own); each Magic Missile dart, Radiant Motes mote and Eldritch Blast beam counts on its own. The DC counts the damage taken before temporary hit points absorb any of it, and monsters' concentration follows the same rule. Dropping to 0 hit points still ends concentration at once.
- Damage to a Wild Shape form now calls for concentration saving throws for the druid's spell (the form saves while it stands, the druid once it drops, against the whole hit); before, only damage carried over to the druid did, and a form dropping to exactly 0 hit points called for none. New setting, Gameplay > Rules (off by default), to keep the old behaviour.
- Damage absorbed by temporary hit points now counts in full for concentration saving throws; before, a hit they partly absorbed counted only what got through, and one they absorbed exactly called for no save. New setting, Gameplay > Rules (off by default), to count only damage to hit points.
- New setting, Gameplay > Rules (on with the 2024 rules): the DC of concentration saving throws is capped at 30.
- Concentration saves no longer get bonuses tied to the damage type: Dwarven Resilience, the Belt of Dwarvenkind and similar poison advantages used to apply to concentration saves for poison damage. War Caster and other concentration bonuses still apply.
- Not tested in game: all of the above; not tested on two computers.
