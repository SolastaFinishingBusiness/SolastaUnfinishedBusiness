- Saves made with Finishing Business can load in Solasta without the mod, as long as they use nothing the mod adds (issue #38).
- Spell points: each hero switches between spell slots and spell points at its next completed long rest after the setting changes, and only then. A hero using slots no longer carries the hidden spell-point pool, which on its own stopped a save loading without the mod. Saves from older versions follow the current setting on their first load.
- A Monk's level 2 Patient Defense used to be saved under a name only the mod knows, which on its own stopped a save loading without the mod. While it is the standard one it is now saved under the game's own name, and the mod restores its version on load. A Monk using the 2024 Focus options still needs the mod.
- The Save and Load screens show a small badge on each save made with this version: the Finishing Business medallion when the save needs the mod, the game's own Solasta icon when no dependency on modded content was found, and nothing when it can't tell (older saves, and saves in Dungeon Maker campaigns or locations unless they need the mod). A hero with more than one class, or above level 16, counts as needing the mod. Hover for details.
- Dungeon Maker: a "Requires Finishing Business" toggle on the campaign editor. The mod's campaign commands run only in campaigns saved with this toggle on in this version or later; elsewhere those variables are ordinary variables. Saving a campaign that has command-like variables asks once, with a "Don't ask again" box. Saves made in such a campaign need the mod.
- The title screen's Finishing Business badge has a new look.
- Saves made in Save by Location folders aren't listed by the game without the mod; move them to the main save folder first.
- Multiplayer: every player needs this same version. Older versions don't send the heroes' new casting and Monk information, and this version expects it.
- Tested in game in single player: the switch between spell slots and spell points at a long rest, the save badges, the Dungeon Maker toggle and its question on saving, and a save made at the default settings loading in Solasta without the mod (on a build before the final save checks). Not tested in game: loading in Solasta without the mod with this version's save checks, a Monk's Patient Defense without the mod, the save of a multiclass hero or of a Dungeon Maker location, two computers, and a gamepad.
