- Way of Survival: Physical Perfection now needs a ki point; it no longer revives the monk for free.
- School of Abjuration: cantrips no longer create or feed the Arcane Ward; in BG3 mode a spell adds its level (not twice it); Projected Ward is no longer offered on a critical miss; the Abjuration Savant text says Abjuration.
- Oath of the Motherland: Flames of the Motherland's retaliation now deals fire damage.
- Oath of Devotion: Holy Nimbus now gives advantage on any saving throw against spells cast by fiends or undead.
- Way of the Light: Purity of Light now restores 4 hit points (allies half that).
- Domain of Oblivion: Keeper of Oblivion only uses its once-per-turn heal for an enemy you can see dying within 30 feet.
- Champion: Survivor now also heals at exactly half your hit points.
- Rogues: Opportunist, Gloomblade, Shadow Dance, Cunning Strike and the Duelist's Bravado now need a weapon your Sneak Attack can use; Bravado also needs a melee hit.
- Swashbuckler: Panache now makes its target immune for an hour, reports a failed contest, ends with the battle (no more errors after a fight) and shows its name in the log.
- Eldritch Versatility: Battlefield Shorthand only copies a spell on a successful Arcana check, and each cast of a copied spell repeats the check instead of always removing it.
- Metamagic: Powerful Spell now costs 1 sorcery point per spell level.
- Arcane Fighter: the extra 1d8 now applies to weapon attacks only.
- War Magic: Power Surge is only regained when Counterspell actually ends the spell.
- Descriptions now match what these do: Cloak of Shadows (short rest), Psion Telekinetic push and pull (1 sorcery point), Raven's Deadly Focus (reroll with advantage), Umbral Soul (twice your rogue level), Eye of the Storm (lightning).
- Swashbuckler: Panache's contest is now one combat log line with both rolls and their modifiers (the losing check no longer reads "Success"), reactions that change ability checks can now be used on it, and the power has an icon. A tie still goes to the target.
- Swashbuckler: the two Rakish Audacity features are now titled "Rakish Audacity (Initiative)" and "Rakish Audacity (Sneak Attack)".
- Not tested in game: all of the above.
