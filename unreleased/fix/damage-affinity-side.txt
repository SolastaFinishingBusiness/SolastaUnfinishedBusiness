- Features that let your damage ignore a resistance or immunity no longer also remove your own when you are the one hit. Elemental Adept and Elemental Master kept stripping the hero's own resistance or immunity to the chosen type (so Elemental Master's granted resistance never worked); the same happened with Poisoner, the Defiler's Beacons of Corruption, the Vitriolist's Arsenal, the Oath of Hatred's Ardent Hate and the level 17-20 Rising Dawn. Your own resistances now always apply when you take damage.
- Elemental Adept and Elemental Master: only spells ignore the chosen type's resistance or immunity, as described. Weapon attacks and powers of that type used to ignore it too.
- Devastating Strikes: after a melee critical hit, only that attack ignores the target's resistances, as described. Until the hero's next attack, any other damage it dealt (a spell, for example) ignored them too, and its own resistances were stripped if it was hit.
- Elemental Bane: the marked creature loses its resistance to the chosen type when it is hit, as before, but the damage it deals of that type no longer ignores its victims' resistance.
- Hellwalker ranger, Mark of the Dammed: the ranger's fire damage now ignores the marked creature's fire resistance, as described. It used to do nothing to marked creatures, and instead removed the ranger's own fire immunity when a marked creature hit the ranger.
- Features that let your damage ignore a resistance (Elemental Adept, Elemental Bane and the others above) now also ignore one granted by an Inventor's Resistant Armor infusion. It was added after those features had run, so it always applied.
- Not tested in game: Elemental Adept and Elemental Master spells against a resistant or immune creature, and weapon attacks of the type not ignoring it; Devastating Strikes; Elemental Bane; Mark of the Dammed; Poisoner, Beacons of Corruption, Vitriolic Arsenal, Ardent Hate and Rising Dawn; a Resistant Armor infusion's resistance against these features; anything on two computers. Tested in single player: a Tiefling with Elemental Adept (fire) keeps its own fire resistance when hit by Burning Hands.
