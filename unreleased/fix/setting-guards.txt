- Monk: the "Self Restoration at level 10" option now decides Self Restoration; the Heightened Focus option decided it.
- Monk, Perfect Focus: now works without the Uncanny Metabolism option, as described ("When you roll Initiative and don't use Uncanny Metabolism...").
- Ranger, Relentless Hunter: Hunter's Mark loses its concentration only with the sub-option "Remove concentration requirements from Hunter's Mark instead"; otherwise damage just can't break it. From level 13 it never needed concentration, whichever way the sub-option was set.
- "Applies 1d4 damage on failed jump checks" now turns that damage on and off; it came with the "encumbrance and armor penalties to jump range" option instead.
- Channel Divinity on a short rest: a character whose Channel Divinity class follows the 2014 rules gets every use back again, whichever 2024 options are on; with only one class's 2024 option on, the other class got nothing back. A Paladin and Cleric with both 2024 options gets one use back, not two.
- Removed the setting "Allow features that move self or allies to include mid-air positions if that creature is flying" (Gameplay > Campaigns). Its text said the opposite of what it did, and each value only took something away. Instinctive Pounce, Second Wind's move, Withdraw, Remarkable Athlete, Maneuvering Attack and Strategic Reposition now offer the spots the game's own position picker would: any height within reach, a creature that walks on ground only, a flying creature in mid-air too. Before, the setting off kept the move at the creature's own height, and on made flying creatures land.
- New setting in its place, "Allow walls as destinations for creatures that can move on walls" (Gameplay > Campaigns, on by default): a creature that can move on walls, for example with Spider Climb, can also be sent to a climbable wall by those six features, as the game's own movement lets it.
- Spirit Walker: the prompt says the Spirit Guardians last as long as the rage when Persistent Rage 2024 is on, which is what makes them last; it followed the Relentless Rage option.
- Spare the Dying: the 2024 option shows the 2024 description and the game's own shows without it; they were swapped.
- "Add senses to monsters": Aksha keeps its removed Tremorsense removed; it got it back on every perception check.
- Turning "Background ASI" off gives Humans their racial ability score increase back (or Alternate Human's point pool); it stayed removed.
- OpenAI translation: "Top K" is sent with the request when set above 0 (OpenAI's own API doesn't accept it; OpenAI-compatible servers do). It was never sent.
- Not tested in game: any of these, and anything on two computers.
