- Casting panel: its spell rows are now laid out from what each spell list can cast on that panel, with multi-line spell offering on or off. Lines with nothing to cast are left out, so a bonus-action panel no longer draws empty rows whose headers overlap (for example FAIRY and SORCERER over a Fairy Sorcerer's only bonus-action spell) or an empty WARLOCK header. With multi-line spell offering, the Warlock's pact slots now show on its first row (they were missing). Without it, every spell list's levels above the first row get a row of their own, so a spell list's higher spells are no longer left off when another list also has spells that high. Hidden spell lists are left out in both layouts.
- Not tested in game: two spell lists with spells above the first row, controller navigation, multiclass Warlock pact slots, Mystic Arcanum, Blast Reload, Way of the Light Monk, Magic Initiate, Touched, multiplayer.
