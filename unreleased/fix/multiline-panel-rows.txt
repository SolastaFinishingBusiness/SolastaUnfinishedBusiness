- Casting panel: its spell rows are now laid out from what each spell list can cast on that panel, with multi-line spell offering on or off. Lines with nothing to cast are left out, so a bonus-action panel no longer draws empty rows whose headers overlap (for example FAIRY and SORCERER over a Fairy Sorcerer's only bonus-action spell) or an empty WARLOCK header. With multi-line spell offering, the Warlock's pact slots now show on its first row (they were missing). Without it, every spell list's levels above the first row get a row of their own, so a spell list's higher spells are no longer left off when another list also has spells that high. Hidden spell lists are left out in both layouts.
- Casting panel: with multi-line spell offering on or off, a row of spells that would push the panel's CANCEL button past the right edge of the screen (for example a multiclass Paladin and Warlock with spell points, without multi-line spell offering) now wraps between spell lists, or between a list's spell levels, onto another row above it, to keep CANCEL on screen where possible. A single spell level too wide for the screen stays as it is.
- Casting panel without multi-line spell offering: a spell list with nothing to cast on the panel no longer shortens the first row (for example a Wizard's first row ending at level III instead of IV because of a racial spell list that isn't shown).
- Casting panel: a row shorter than the panel's widest row no longer spreads its spell lists apart with gaps between them; every row is packed from the left.
- Casting panel: a row that only holds a spell list's slot box (the Warlock's pact slots) now takes the box's height, so the box no longer covers the row above it.
- Not tested in game: a row that only holds the Warlock's pact slot box, other screen resolutions and interface scales, a controller (its button hints beside the panel, navigation), a single spell list too wide for the screen, two spell lists with spells above the first row, Mystic Arcanum, Blast Reload, Way of the Light Monk, Magic Initiate, Touched, the reaction panel, multiplayer.
