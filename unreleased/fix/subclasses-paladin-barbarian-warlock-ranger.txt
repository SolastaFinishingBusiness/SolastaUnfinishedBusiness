- Demon Slayer: Judgment Mark now really gives back a use of Channel Divinity when a marked enemy dies (in the Paladin's own pool with the 2024 rules).
- Scornful Prayer (Oath of Hatred) and Infectious Fury (Path of the Beast) now do nothing on a successful saving throw.
- Oath of Dread: Aura of Domination now deals psychic damage, as described.
- Oath of the Ancients: Aura of Warding now gives resistance only to the damage of the spell that triggers it, not to every hit until your next turn; Nature's Wrath now reaches 10 feet.
- Archfey: Misty Escape now uses your reaction and needs one.
- Oath of Thunder: Storm Herald now strikes a 3x3 area (15 feet), and its descriptions give distances in feet.
- Path of the Elements: Elemental Conduit shows its own description, and the Storm and Blizzard damage is no longer called Wildfire.
- Ranges now match their descriptions: Searing Vengeance 30 feet, Barrier of Stone 30 feet, Soul Hex 30 feet, Wild Surge teleport 30 feet.
- Sky Warrior: Death from Above now follows melee weapon attacks only, and Ghostly Howl is offered only against an enemy's hit.
- Celestial: Healing Light can always heal at least one die. Battlerager: Reckless Abandon gives at least 1 temporary hit point.
- Cloak of Flies (Pernicious Cloak) now recharges on a short or long rest.
- Descriptions: Blessed Glow says once per short rest and Spiritual Pact Weapon says a bonus action (what they do); the Paladin level 18 Aura of Courage and Aura of Protection descriptions are no longer swapped.
